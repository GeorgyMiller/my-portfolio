About the Project

Organizing Clothing Store Operations

The Clothing Store Management System is designed to support the day-to-day operations of a clothing business. It focuses on maintaining product records, monitoring available stock, and recording sales.

By keeping product and transaction information organized, the system aims to make it easier for authorized staff to review inventory and maintain accurate store records.
